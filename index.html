Five questions before the bot answers

Ask them in this order. The first yes decides the route. If all five are no, the bot answers, from an article that has an owner and a review date.

  1. Is money or account access at risk right now?

    Unknown login, withdrawal the user didn't make, 2FA changed, a shared password or seed phrase, a "support" contact asking to verify a wallet.

    Security P1. A person replies first. The bot only gives safety steps while the user waits.

  2. Is there a review, a legal request or a fraud report?

    Restricted account, source of funds request, police or court letter, "tell me who owns this address".

    Compliance The reply is neutral and the same every time. No reasons, no hints, no promises about dates.

  3. Does the fix need someone to do something in the back office?

    Manual credit, missing memo or tag, wrong-network recovery, change of phone or email, refund.

    Agent With the evidence already collected, so the user doesn't repeat the story.

  4. Is this person vulnerable, under pressure or making a formal complaint?

    Large share of savings, someone else directing them, distress, "I'll go to the regulator", or simply asking for a person.

    Agent A senior one. Formal complaints get logged with their deadline.

  5. Has the bot already failed twice on this issue?

    Same question again, user rephrasing, or "that didn't help".

    Agent The bot says it's handing over and passes its summary.
